## Section3: Details of the household members

### OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

- To record basic demographics on gender, age, relationship to head of household, marital status, education level, no. of days stayed away from home, no. of meals usually taken for each member of the household listed in Section 2.

### IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

- All the household members of the selected household will be listed and their related information will be recorded.
• Total number of members listed in Section3 will be same as household size in Section2.
