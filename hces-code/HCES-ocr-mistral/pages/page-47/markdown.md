## Section 12: Consumption of pan, tobacco and intoxicants during the last 7 days

### OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

This section is for recording the quantity and value of consumption of pan, tobacco and intoxicants by the household during the last 7 days preceding the date of the survey.
